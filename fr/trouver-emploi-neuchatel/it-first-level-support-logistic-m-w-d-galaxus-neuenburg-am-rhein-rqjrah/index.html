Description complète

We are looking for a dedicated and resilient IT First-Level Supporter for our logistics site in Neuenburg am Rhein.Working in a two-shift model (05:00–23:00 / Saturday 05:00–18:00), you’ll troubleshoot IT issues, support employees and keep our operations running smoothly.Ready to make an impact? Join us and help shape our new logistics site!

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Neuenburg am Rhein, Hans-Buck-Straße 3, 7939 Neuenburg am Rhein

Key information

We welcome all applications, but can only consider applications submitted online. Applications submitted by post will be disposed of for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • First point of contact for all IT hardware incidents on the logistics floor
  • Share knowledge and procedures with logistics employees during troubleshooting, so recurring issues can be solved independently
  • Resolve incidents directly on-site or guide staff to the correct escalation path
  • Act as on-site liaison for external IT partners during Pickett/Blocker emergencies
  • Procure IT hardware for new and existing workstations Set up workstations including physical cabling and network connection
  • Replace defective hardware
  • Collaborate with 2nd Level Support (System Engineers / ICT): escalate, implement, and validate proposed solution

Requisiti

  • 2 Years: IT or similar
  • Basic understanding of IT hardware, networks, and IT systems in a logistics environment
  • Practical experience with endpoint devices: scanners, label printers, handheld terminals, industrial hardware
  • Ability to perform diagnostics, resets, cabling, and simple configurations
  • Comfortable learning new tools quickly — formal deep expertise is less important than curiosity and adaptability
  • A background or education in IT (e.g., Fachinformatiker/in) is an advantage, but not mandatory
  • You build trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • You use creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • No qualifications/further training required
  • German (Good knowledge)
  • English (Basic knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Values, not empty words: Cooperative, innovative, piratesque, responsible, ambitious – these five values are what we practice and preach.
  • Courageous thinking: We encourage everyone to think on their feet, and love it when they come up with bold or edgy ideas.
  • Uncomplicated approach: Simplicity is at our core. We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what really matters – from overcoming challenges to celebrating our shared successes.

  • More Benefits Germany: More Benefits Germany: 30 days of holiday, exclusive employee conditions in our online shop, a shuttle service (for Neuenburg am Rhein) and other benefits.

Le type de contrat affiché ici provient directement de l'annonce originale de l'employeur ; confirmez toujours les horaires exacts, le préavis et la durée d'essai avec Galaxus pendant le processus de candidature, ces détails pouvant varier même au sein de la même catégorie de contrat.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Neuchâtel ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Neuchâtel après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Neuchâtel ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
