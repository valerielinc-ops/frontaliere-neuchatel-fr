Your mission

CSEM develops innovative wireless, sensing and high-frequency technologies that require reliable experimental characterisation from RF frequencies up to the sub-THz range. The objective of this internship is to develop, assemble and validate measurement benches for RF components, antennas, materials and advanced electromagnetic structures. The work will combine hands-on laboratory activities, RF instrumentation, calibration, measurement automation, data processing and comparison with electromagnetic simulations. Duration: 4 to 6 months

Your responsibilities

The student will first become familiar with the available RF, mmWave and sub-THz instrumentation, laboratory safety rules, calibration methods and existing measurement facilities. After this initial training phase, the student will contribute to several of the following activities:

  • Develop and align free-space a sub-THz measurement setup for the characterisation of materials and metasurfaces.
  • Design and assemble an RF measurement bench for active RF components.
  • Contribute to antenna characterisation activities.
  • Automate instrument control, data acquisition, processing and report generation.
  • Assess measurement repeatability and uncertainty, compare experimental results with electromagnetic simulations, and document the developed setups and procedures.

Your profile Know-how

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor or Master’s degree in electrical engineering, telecommunications, microwave engineering, applied physics or a related field.
  • Foundations in RF engineering, electromagnetics.
  • Interest in hands-on laboratory work, RF instrumentation, calibration and experimental troubleshooting.
  • Experience with vector network analysers, spectrum analysers, signal generators, power meters or antenna measurements is an advantage.
  • Experience with electromagnetic simulation tools such as Ansys HFSS or CST Studio Suite is an advantage.
  • Programming skills in Python for instrument control and data processing.

Interpersonal skills

  • Curious, methodical, autonomous and comfortable working in a multidisciplinary environment.
  • Good problem-solving abilities and a hands-on, results-oriented approach.
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills.
  • Good written and spoken English
  • French is an advantage.
